# Project Brief: Team Bingo Challenge
*BMAD Phase 1 — Analyst*

## Problem Statement
The product design and marketing organizations don't know each other well. People stick to their own teams at events, and cross-functional relationships form slowly. We need a structured but fun activity that gives people a reason to approach strangers and start real conversations.

## Proposed Solution
A "human bingo" icebreaker game. Each player gets a unique 5x5 bingo card. Every square holds a prompt describing a person ("Has shipped a product in another country", "Ran a marketing campaign that flopped — and can laugh about it"). Players mingle, find a colleague who matches a prompt, and write that person's name in the square. First to complete a row, column, or diagonal shouts "BINGO!"

## Target Users
- **Players**: ~20–100 people across product design and marketing. Mixed seniority. Will play on their phones or on printed cards.
- **Facilitator**: The organizer (event host) who kicks off the game, explains rules, and awards prizes.

## Goals
1. Every player talks to at least 5 people they didn't know before.
2. Conversations go beyond name/title — prompts should spark stories.
3. Zero setup friction: no accounts, no installs, works instantly.

## Key Constraints
- Must work with no backend and no login (privacy + zero friction).
- Cards must be randomized so neighbors can't copy each other.
- Must be usable on a phone AND printable for people who prefer paper.
- Prompts must be inclusive — no prompts that require drinking, physical ability, parenthood, etc.

## Success Metrics
- Time-to-first-card under 10 seconds.
- At least one BINGO within a 30-minute session.
- Facilitator can run the whole thing from one link or one print job.
